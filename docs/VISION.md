# Vision

Independent contractors should be able to run and grow their businesses with
the same connective tissue that large firms have: a bench of trusted peers,
shared equipment, overflow labor, and a reputation that travels with them —
without giving up their independence.

## Principles

- **Local first.** Paradise is valuable when the people near you are on it. We
  grow county by county, starting with Lancaster County, PA.
- **Organizations, not just people.** A contractor is a business. Accounts,
  data, and reputation belong to organizations that can grow from one owner to a crew.
- **Trust is earned and visible.** Licence, insurance, completed exchanges and
  referrals become trust signals over time.
- **Value moves fairly.** Paradise Points let contractors trade help without
  cash friction. Points only move once a secure, auditable ledger exists.
- **Tools that pay for themselves.** Later ecosystems (Business, Media) earn
  their place by saving contractors time and winning them work.

## The five ecosystems

1. **Paradise Network** — profiles, localized maps, professional trust, referrals.
2. **Paradise Exchange** — equipment rentals, labor exchange, materials,
   subcontracting requests, and the Paradise Points system.
3. **Paradise Business** — CRM, estimating, scheduling, customer management, operations.
4. **Paradise Media** — marketing, project portfolios, contractor promotion.
5. **Maestro Integration** — apprenticeships, mentorship, workforce development,
   operated with a separate nonprofit organization.
